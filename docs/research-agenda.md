# Research agenda

## Central question
**How do berth capacity and arrival/service patterns affect vessel waiting time under a simple earliest-available scheduling policy?**

## Hypotheses
### H1
Additional berths reduce waiting time with diminishing returns.

### H2
Arrival bursts create queueing even when average utilization appears moderate.

### H3
Alternative priority rules can reduce tail waiting time while increasing average delay for other vessels.

## Experiments
1. Sweep berth count under fixed vessel sets.
2. Generate Poisson-like arrival streams and variable service durations.
3. Compare FCFS/earliest-available with future priority and optimization baselines.

## Metrics
- average wait
- maximum wait
- berth utilization
- makespan
- tail wait percentile
